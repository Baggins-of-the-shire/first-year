First Year

My wife and I call our neighborhood the Shire, and most days it earns the name. This is the record of my first year of walking it slowly, pond bank to wood edge to back lawn, and stooping to see what’s come up overnight. Most of it is small: mushrooms the size of a shirt button, shelf fungus climbing a trunk like stairs. If you go out with a little intention, something is always growing, even in a mowed yard.
