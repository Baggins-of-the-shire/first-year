First Year

My wife and I call our neighborhood the Shire, and most days it earns the name. This is the record of my first year of walking it slowly — pond bank, wood edge, back lawn — and stooping to see what’s come up overnight. Most of it is small: mushrooms the size of a shirt button, shelf fungus climbing a trunk like stairs. If you go out with a little intention, something is always growing, even in a mowed yard. Newest finds first; nothing gets a name unless I’m sure of it.
